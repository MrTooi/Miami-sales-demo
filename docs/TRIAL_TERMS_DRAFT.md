## MargenX free trial terms

**Between** `[PROVIDER]` ("MargenX", "we") **and** `[SHOP]`, through `[NAMED PERSON]` ("you").

**1. What the trial is.** A free, private trial of MargenX for one shop and one named person, by invitation. It runs for 14 calendar dates, from `[START DATE]` (the day access is switched on) to the closeout at `[CLOSEOUT DATE AND TIME, MIAMI]`. Only your exact work email is invited. Please don't share your login or let anyone else use it.

**2. What MargenX shows.** For the file you upload, MargenX shows **sales minus direct job costs**, by job and by customer, exact to the cent. **It is not net profit**: it leaves out overhead, taxes and anything not in your file. Every figure says whether it is **Complete** or **Needs a check**. "Needs a check" means MargenX couldn't prove the figure from your file, and says why. **Complete means MargenX's required checks and your confirmations are satisfied. It doesn't guarantee that nothing was left out of, or recorded wrongly in, QuickBooks** (section 3). **Many figures may show Needs a check, even when you follow the export steps exactly.** That's MargenX refusing to guess, not an error.

**3. What MargenX can and can't check.** MargenX reads one supported file: QuickBooks Online's **Profit and Loss Detail**, exported to Excel on the accrual basis, plus optionally **Sales by Customer Detail** for the same dates (the guide gives the steps). Other files are refused with a plain message. **MargenX can only check what the file itself shows.** QuickBooks can filter a report in ways it doesn't print in the file, for example leaving out an account or a customer. MargenX can't see that, so please follow the export steps exactly. **So MargenX asks you:** after its questions about your cost accounts, it lists the cost accounts it found in your file, with their totals, and asks you to confirm that the file includes your whole business's income and costs for those dates, with no filters excluding accounts or transactions. Until you answer "Yes, I checked", every figure shows Needs a check. **MargenX relies on your confirmation for that file,** as it says with your results: "MargenX calculates figures from your uploaded reports and your answers. QuickBooks exports may not reveal filters that excluded accounts or transactions. We therefore ask you to check the export settings and confirm its coverage. Complete means our required checks and confirmations are satisfied; it does not guarantee that nothing was omitted or incorrectly recorded in QuickBooks. If you are unsure, choose Not sure." **MargenX also asks about your job costs:** it shows how it counted them, and asks you to confirm that every job cost for those dates is in the report, on its job. Until you answer yes, job costs and profit after job costs show Needs a check. **MargenX relies on that confirmation too,** as it says with your results: "For jobs and customers, Complete also relies on your confirmation that every job cost for these dates is in this report and on its job. MargenX can't check that against your payroll or your bills." The check we did together at our meeting covered only the export we saw that day.

**4. What to leave out, and your consent.** Upload only the supported report. Don't upload, type or send us bank or card details, tax ID numbers, or individual payroll records. **You confirm you're allowed to share this report with us** for this trial. Before you can add a file, MargenX shows its consent wording, and you press **"I agree – let me add my report"**. That consent is for the one browser tab you press it in, and only while it's the only MargenX tab open; no other tab can add files.

**5. Where your file goes, and what clears it.** Your file travels over an encrypted connection through **Cloudflare** (which runs the login and the connection) to a server run by **Render** (which hosts MargenX's copy for your shop). Both act for us as service providers. MargenX reads your file **in memory only**: it doesn't save it to a disk or a database, keeps no backups, and doesn't put your file's contents in its logs. Your copy of MargenX is yours alone during the trial, and is stopped at the end. **What we can't guarantee:** Cloudflare and Render keep their own records of logins and connections (for example your email, times and internet address), under their own terms; and we can't control what your own browser, computer or screenshots keep. **Any of these clears your file and results from MargenX at once**, by ending MargenX's whole session for your copy and starting a fresh, empty one: pressing **End session**; pressing **Log out**; closing or reloading the tab; a dropped connection (for example a Wi-Fi drop); your login ending; and a file that takes MargenX more than a minute to read. Your page then reloads by itself, and you add your file again if you want to continue. **An upload already on its way** can keep arriving for up to 2 minutes before it is cut off; if your session ends meanwhile, it is thrown away unread. Results already on your screen, and anything you copied, downloaded or screenshotted, stay with you.

**6. Logging out.** The Log out button ends your MargenX session straight away, once MargenX has confirmed your login, and clears your file and results (section 5). For safety, a new login within about half a minute of logging out (or of MargenX restarting unexpectedly) may be refused: MargenX then shows a **Log in again** button; if it shows again, wait half a minute and try once more. If it can't confirm it in time (for example while Cloudflare's login service is slow), it says "Not allowed." and nothing is logged out: press Log out again, and if it keeps failing, close the browser and tell us. The login itself may take a short moment to end everywhere (Cloudflare documents a short delay). Closing the tab doesn't log you out.

**7. The closeout, and ending early.** At the closeout, we stop your copy of MargenX. Stopping cuts the server off: any open tab loses its connection, any upload in progress is ended, and everything held in memory is cleared. Figures already on your screen may stay visible there, and anything you saved or screenshotted stays with you. Either of us may end the trial earlier, at any time, by telling the other; we then stop your copy the same way. Nothing brings an ended trial back.

**8. If something goes wrong.** If we learn of a problem that may have exposed your file or results, we stop your copy at once and tell you within `[INCIDENT NOTICE TIME]`, with what we know. If you notice anything odd, contact us at `[SUPPORT CONTACT]`. We aim to reply within `[REPLY TIME]`. **Please never email us a financial file**: we'll help without it.

**9. Free, with no automatic charge.** The trial is free. We don't ask for payment details, and nothing turns into a paid plan by itself.

**10. No warranty.** MargenX is a trial of software still being built. It's provided "as is". Please check important figures against your own records before relying on them for decisions.
