**What MargenX does.** It shows which of your jobs and customers make or lose money: your **sales minus direct job costs**, exact to the cent, from your own QuickBooks report. It is **not net profit** (overhead, taxes and anything not in the report are left out).

**1. Log in.** Click **Log in** on margenx.org, or go to **app.margenx.org**. Enter your work email (the one we invited). Cloudflare, our login service, emails you a one-time code. Type it in. There's no password.

**2. Export your report from QuickBooks Online** (on your own computer):
1. **Reports** → search for **Profit and Loss Detail** → open it.
2. **Report period:** the dates you want to look at, for example last quarter.
3. **Accounting method: Accrual.** (Cash basis is refused.)
4. **Don't filter it, and don't change its columns.** Leave every customer and every account in. A filter QuickBooks doesn't print in the file can't be seen by MargenX, and could make jobs look more profitable than they are.
5. **Run report** → **Export** → **Export to Excel**.
6. **To see job profit as Complete,** also export **Sales by Customer Detail** for **the same dates**, the same way. MargenX uses it to double-check your sales.

**3. Upload it.** Tick the box to say you agree to MargenX reading the file, then drag in the file (or both files). MargenX reads them in memory only and doesn't save them. It may ask a few questions, for example whether an account is a job cost or overhead, and whether you exported both reports with no filter.

**What to leave out.** Upload only the two QuickBooks reports above: Profit and Loss Detail, and optionally Sales by Customer Detail. Never upload or type bank or card details, tax ID numbers, or individual payroll records.

**4. Read the results.**
- Each figure says **Complete** or **Needs a check**. "Needs a check" means MargenX couldn't prove it from your file, and it says why and how much money is involved.
- **Expect many figures to say Needs a check,** even when you follow the steps exactly. MargenX would rather say so than guess.
- Profit by **job** needs both reports and your "no filter" answer before it can be Complete.

**5. When you're done for the day.**
- **End session** clears your files and results from MargenX.
- **Log out** ends your login. It may take a short moment to end everywhere.
- Anything you save, download or screenshot stays on your own device.

**6. The end of your trial.** Your trial ends at `[CLOSEOUT DATE AND TIME, MIAMI]`. Then we stop your copy of MargenX: open tabs lose their connection, and MargenX keeps nothing you uploaded. (Our login and hosting services, Cloudflare and Render, keep their own records of logins and connections, and anything your own browser or computer saved stays with you; the trial terms say more.) If you'd like to go over your results with Alex, book the follow-up meeting **before** that time.
