# MarginX shop conversation guide (one page)

For Alex and Abrey. Use it for **15–20 minute conversations** with owners or managers of small Miami print, sign and apparel shops (2–15 people). These are conversations only: **no files, no photos of screens, no taking their data.**

## What we want to learn
1. Do they actually **care** which jobs and customers make or lose money, or not?
2. What **software** do they use, and what do their exports look like?
3. Would they **try a free pilot**, and later **pay**?

Learning that they *don't* care is just as useful as a yes. Write it down the same way.

## Opening (30 seconds)
> "We're building a simple tool for print and sign shops. You give it the sales and cost reports you already export from QuickBooks or Square, and it shows which jobs and customers actually make you money and which lose it. We're talking to a few Miami shops before we finish it. Can I ask you a few questions about how you handle this today?"

**Don't promise** prices, dates, savings, features or integrations. If they ask, say: "We're still deciding that. That's why we're asking you."

## Questions (ask in this order; skip what doesn't fit)
**Their problem**
1. How do you know today whether a job made money?
2. Tell me about the last job that lost money without you noticing at first. What happened?
3. Which customers do you suspect aren't worth it? How would you check?

**How they work** (this decides what we build next)

4. What do you use for quotes, invoices and bills: QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, Square, spreadsheets, something else?
5. Do your invoices **and** your bills/expenses carry a job or work-order number?
6. Do you track labor or staff hours per job? Where?
7. Do you buy materials into inventory, or expense them right away?
8. Can you export your invoices and bills to CSV or Excel? *(Ask them to describe it or point at the screen. Don't take a copy, not even with names changed.)*
9. Do you have a bookkeeper or accountant? Would they need to be involved?
10. English, Spanish or both?

**Value**

11. If you could see profit per job every week, what would you do differently?
12. Would you try it free if we came back with it on a laptop and went through your own reports together, about an hour?
13. If it helped, what would it be worth per month? *(Only write a number if they give one. Never suggest one.)*

## Ending
- Thank them. If they said yes to question 12, agree on how to reach them and roughly when.
- Never say the pilot is booked or that their data is "safe to share" yet. A real visit needs Alex's written OK first.

## Write it down right after (one note per conversation)
Use the issue #30 format. **Keep what they said separate from what you think it means.**

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Who | "Owner, sign shop, ~6 people, Doral" (no names needed) |
| What they said (their words) | "I only find out a job lost money when the accountant does taxes." |
| What we think it means | "Job profit is invisible to them during the year." |
| Problem | Can't see job profit until year end |
| Software | QuickBooks Online; job no. on invoices only |
| Pilot? | Yes / no / maybe, and when |
| Would pay? | Their number, or "didn't say" |
| Supports MarginX? | yes / no / unclear |
| How sure are you? | 0.0–1.0 |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |

**Rules:**
- Never invent or "round up" a quote, price or number.
- Write down the "no"s and complaints too.
- No customer names, amounts or job details from their business in the notes.
- Send the notes to Alex the same day.
